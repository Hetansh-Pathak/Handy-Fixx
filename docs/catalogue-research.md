Lights, Wiring… Each main section ends with a **check-up visit** (a pro comes, finds the problem and quotes).

## How our base price is set

HandyFix serves tier-2 Gujarat cities, so our catalogue ("Starts at") price is **about 15% below** their Ahmedabad price,
snapped to a clean price ending in 9 that stays **between 10% and 20% below** theirs (₹49, ₹99, ₹149 … in ₹50 steps from
₹200 up; ₹59, ₹69 … in ₹10 steps below ₹200). Check-up visits use the trade's consultation price (₹149 → ₹129).
A pro's own rate still has to stay inside the admin price band; these are catalogue prices only.
Durations are theirs when shown; otherwise ours (*est.*). Very short durations ("5 mins" tap repair) are raised to a
realistic home-visit time.

**Estimates:** rows with no reference price (Home Help, air cooler, pest inspection, painting estimate visit) are
HandyFix estimates for tier-2 Gujarat and are marked *estimate*. Confirm them with local pros before launch.
